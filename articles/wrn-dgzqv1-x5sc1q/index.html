Helezwar Mîtanî, head of the Rojhilat Region Military Press Center, shared information about an attack targeting fighters from the Qamishlo Brigade.

Helezwar Mîtanî said an attack was carried out against the position of brigade fighters in the village of Tewil Herb, Qamishlo, and that the fighters responded.

Mîtanî said two brigade fighters were killed, and two others wounded in the attack, adding that efforts to locate the attackers were continuing.
