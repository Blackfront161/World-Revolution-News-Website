The protests were called by the National Union of Students and buoyed by a big turn out of high school students, who skipped school classes to attend the rally.

The impressive turnout, particularly of high school students, reflects the anger among many young people that Hanson’s far-right politics are getting a hearing in the corporate media, and in-turn being reflected in high polling figures across the country.

While One Nation has been consistently polling between 25-30%, its support among “Gen Z”, 14–29-year-olds, is around 10%.
