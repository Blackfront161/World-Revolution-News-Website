Gerîla TV (

gerillatv.net

), which operates as HPG’s video broadcasting channel, will continue its activities with a renewed broadcasting infrastructure and content structure.

A promotional statement issued by the channel said that Gerîla TV broadcasts footage related to the activities of groups described as the Kurdistan Freedom Guerrillas, as well as documentaries, cultural content, historical programs, and commemorative content about members who have lost their lives.

Gerîla TV stated that the footage it broadcasts is recorded directly by cameras in the field and therefore presents its content “from the primary source.” The channel broadcasts mainly in Kurdish, while also producing content in other languages when necessary.

In the renewed broadcasting period, greater emphasis will be placed on archiving and preserving older footage, as well as producing new digital content. The channel says that through this work, it aims to pass on the memory, life, and historical experience of the guerrilla movement to future generations.

Gerîla TV noted that efforts to collect, archive, and make available to the public footage and documents from the areas where events take place will continue. The channel said that in the new period, it will continue its work without interruption to produce, document, and publish original content.
