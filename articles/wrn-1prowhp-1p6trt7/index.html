30th July, Iceland.

At this stage, we have not received any anonymous communiques about the action.

According to local press, Dalsbú mink farm in Mosfellsdalur, Iceland, was raided overnight releasing all mink. Dalsbú was the last remaining operative mink farm in the country.

The owner of the farm explained that it would’ve taken about two hours to release the mink (8,000 of the 12,000 in the farm) and that due to loss of breeding records, the raid has had a devastating impact.

He also called the activists ‘terrorists’, especially ‘terrorists for the animals’ as ‘they have caused enormous suffering to the mink’.

May the mink (and the raider!) run fast and free!
