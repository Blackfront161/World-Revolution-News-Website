This joint webinar with the Academy of European Law (ERA) and the University of Bristol covers everything from key terms and concepts to the most recent legal developments of trans rights in Europe. Participants will hear from leading experts about EU laws, how European courts affect trans rights, and practical ways to support trans people within legal and institutional systems.

Combining structured presentations with discussions and impulse sessions, the programme provides a balanced mix of theory, policy insights, and hands-on guidance to build practical skills for navigating this fast-changing area.

About the webinar

Date and Time: 07 – 08 December 2026, 09 – 13:00 CET, online

Target audience: trans activists, LGBTI rights advocates, journalists, academics, paralegals, lawyers, judges, legal staff of ombudspersons and NGOs, policymakers and civil servants.

This event is free to attend due to the generous co-financing of the UKRI Futures Leaders Fellowship .

Deadline to apply: 01 December

Note: Registration spots are limited, and participants will be selected. Individuals will not be discriminated in that procedure based on the EU’s protected grounds.

Key topics

Key terms and concepts of trans identities

The basics of EU law on trans rights

How European courts are influencing real-world change

Insights from recent landmark EU court cases
