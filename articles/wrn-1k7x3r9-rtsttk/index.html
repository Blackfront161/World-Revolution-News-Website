Imrali Delegation meets President Erdoğan today

Movement News

Following the adoption of the Framework Law bill by Parliament, DEM Party Imrali Delegation members Pervin Buldan and Mithat Sancar will meet with President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. The meeting is scheduled to take place today at 4 p.m. at the Presidential Palace in Beştepe.
