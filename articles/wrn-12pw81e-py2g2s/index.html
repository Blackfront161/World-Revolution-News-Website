Despite the cold winds, sewage spills and ouch-y pebbles, British people love the beach. But if the beach was that good, surely we wouldn’t have needed to invent claw machines?

In this episode, Nadia, Keir and Jem compare their emotional connections to the seaside – from Southport sands to the Red Sea – and think about why humans are drawn to the border between water and land.

They also talk about the beach in deep-time, the birth of surf culture and why seaside towns vote Reform, with music from 808 State and The B-52’s. Sous les pavés, la plage!
